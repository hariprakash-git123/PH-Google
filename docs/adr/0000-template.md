# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What made a decision necessary. The forces in play — constraints, existing
code, things that were already tried. Written so someone who was not there can
follow it.

## Decision

What we are doing, in a sentence or two.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the part that stops the
decision being re-opened.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes harder, and what has to be true for it to keep
working. Be honest about the downsides; an ADR with none is not a decision.
